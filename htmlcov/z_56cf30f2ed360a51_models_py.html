12def fermi_hubbard_dimer(t: float, u: float) -> FermionOperator: 

13 r"""The two-site Fermi-Hubbard model with spin \(1/2\). 

14 

15 It has four spin-orbital modes, indexed by `mode = 2 * site + spin`, with spin 

16 `0` for up and `1` for down. Site `0` has modes `0` and `1`, and site `1` has 

17 modes `2` and `3`. The Hamiltonian is: 

18 

19 \[ 

20 H = -t \sum_\sigma \big(a^\dagger_{0\sigma} a_{1\sigma} 

21 + a^\dagger_{1\sigma} a_{0\sigma}\big) 

22 + U \big(n_{0\uparrow} n_{0\downarrow} + n_{1\uparrow} n_{1\downarrow}\big) 

23 \] 

24 

25 The ground-state energy at half filling has the closed form 

26 [`fermi_hubbard_dimer_ground_energy()`][eigenkit.chemistry.models.fermi_hubbard_dimer_ground_energy]. 

27 This makes the model an exact check of the fermion-to-qubit mapping and of 

28 VQE, with no external dependency. 

29 

30 Args: 

31 t (float): The hopping amplitude between the two sites. 

32 u (float): The on-site Coulomb repulsion. 

33 

34 Returns: 

35 The Hamiltonian, as a `FermionOperator`. 

36 """ 

37 hamiltonian = FermionOperator.zero() 

38 for spin in (0, 1): 

39 site0, site1 = spin, 2 + spin # 2*0+spin, 2*1+spin 

40 hop = FermionOperator.creation(site0) * FermionOperator.annihilation(site1) 

41 hop = hop + FermionOperator.creation(site1) * FermionOperator.annihilation(site0) 

42 hamiltonian = hamiltonian + (-t) * hop 

43 for site in (0, 1): 

44 up, down = 2 * site, 2 * site + 1 

45 hamiltonian = hamiltonian + u * ( 

46 FermionOperator.number(up) * FermionOperator.number(down) 

47 ) 

48 return hamiltonian

51def fermi_hubbard_dimer_ground_energy(t: float, u: float) -> float: 

52 r"""The ground-state energy of the Fermi-Hubbard dimer at half filling. 

53 

54 The energy of [`fermi_hubbard_dimer()`][eigenkit.chemistry.models.fermi_hubbard_dimer] 

55 with two electrons in a singlet state is \((U - \sqrt{U^2 + 16t^2}) / 2\). This 

56 value is the ground state over all particle numbers only when \(U < 3t\). For a 

57 larger \(U\), double occupancy costs so much energy that the state with a single 

58 electron, of energy \(-t\), is lower, and the ground state over all particle 

59 numbers is \(\min\big(-t, (U - \sqrt{U^2 + 16t^2}) / 2\big)\). An ansatz that 

60 conserves the number of particles, such as UCCSD, targets the half-filling sector 

61 directly. 

62 The hardware-efficient ansatz explores all sectors, so use it only where the 

63 half-filling state is the overall ground state. 

64 

65 Args: 

66 t (float): The hopping amplitude, as in the model. 

67 u (float): The on-site repulsion, as in the model. 

68 

69 Returns: 

70 The ground-state energy at half filling, from the closed form. 

71 """ 

72 return (u - math.sqrt(u**2 + 16.0 * t**2)) / 2.0
